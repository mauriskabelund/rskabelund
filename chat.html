🩺 AI for Medical Students — Quick Tips ▼

Remember: AI is a powerful learning tool, not a replacement for clinical judgment or verified medical resources.

Use AI effectively:
  • Be specific — Include context (patient scenario, learning objective, exam type)
  • Ask for reasoning — "Explain why" helps you learn, not just get answers
  • Verify everything — Cross-reference with UpToDate, textbooks, or faculty
  • Practice clinically — Use AI to quiz yourself, generate differentials, or explain concepts
Don't use AI to:
  • Make real patient care decisions
  • Replace studying primary sources
  • Submit AI-generated work as your own
